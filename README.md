KPMG Data Analytics Project – Sales Analysis for Shiprocket

In this project with KPMG, I conducted an in-depth analysis of the overall sales performance of Shiprocket, a fictional cycle company, using the provided dataset. The primary objective was to assess dataset quality, identify any integrity or reliability issues, and derive meaningful insights to help the company better understand its target customers.

As part of the project, I performed a comprehensive data exploration process to identify trends, patterns, and key performance indicators. By developing a structured approach to model development and interpretation, I analyzed various factors influencing sales and customer preferences. Additionally, I designed an interactive dashboard that summarized key findings, enabling stakeholders to make data-driven decisions effectively.

Through my analysis, I discovered that sales were significantly higher in a particular state, suggesting a strong regional demand for Shiprocket's products. Additionally, I found that the majority of customers purchasing our cycles were female, indicating a potential market segment that could be further targeted for growth. Based on these findings, I provided recommendations on the ideal target customer, helping the company refine its marketing and sales strategies.

The final deliverables included a detailed presentation covering data exploration, model insights, and key interpretations, along with a user-friendly dashboard to visualize and summarize the analysis results. This project showcased my ability to work with real-world data, assess data quality, derive actionable insights, and present findings in a structured and impactful manner.
